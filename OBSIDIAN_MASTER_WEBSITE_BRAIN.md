## Core Principle

Do not build websites from templates.

Build from brand truth, user need, conversion goal, real assets, and careful section-by-section planning.

The AI should behave like a companion during thinking and like a disciplined engineer during execution.

## Knowledge Rule

Books are the deep principle layer.

Videos are the modern execution and taste layer.

The real project context decides which knowledge applies.

Never paste old tactics or trendy visual moves directly into a new project. Translate them through the business, audience, proof, assets, conversion goal, mobile behavior, accessibility, speed, and trust.
