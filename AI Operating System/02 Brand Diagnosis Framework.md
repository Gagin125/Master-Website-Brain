# Brand Diagnosis Framework

Never choose sections, fonts, colors, or animations before diagnosing the brand.

## Required Inputs

Collect:

- business name
- location
- primary action
- services/products
- prices if relevant
- opening hours
- contact links
- booking method
- real photos/videos
- reviews or proof
- competitors or inspiration
- target audience
- constraints and deadline

## Diagnose These

### 1. Business Category

Cafe, restaurant, barbershop, local service, ecommerce, SaaS, portfolio, app, etc.

### 2. Primary User Action

Examples:

- book appointment
- reserve table
- view menu
- call
- visit location
- buy product
- request quote

The primary CTA should dominate the site.

### 3. Audience

Who is likely to visit and what do they care about first?

Examples:

- young men want haircut quality, price, barber choice, and booking speed
- restaurant visitors want atmosphere, menu, location, and reservation
- cafe visitors want vibe, menu, reviews, and location

### 4. Brand Mood

Choose the closest mode:

- luxury editorial
- warm local
- clean modern
- playful youth
- urban street
- traditional craft
- minimal professional
- cinematic premium
- family-friendly

### 5. Asset Truth

Study real photos first.

Ask:

- Are photos dark, bright, colorful, monochrome, candid, polished, messy?
- Do photos show product clearly?
- Do photos support luxury, warmth, speed, craft, family, or personality?
- Are photos strong enough to carry trust without extra sections?

### 6. Existing Identity

Look for:

- logo style
- typography clues
- dominant colors
- interior design
- tone of reviews
- social media style

### 7. Trust Sources

Use only trust signals that improve the site:

- real reviews
- real team photos
- visible results
- ratings
- years in business
- known clients
- certifications

Remove trust sections that feel generic or interrupt flow.

### 8. Design Risk

Identify what would make this site look fake, generic, or AI-made.

Common risks:

- too many cards
- too many badges
- fake luxury gold
- playful UI on a serious brand
- stock photos
- overanimated sections
- sections included only because they are typical

## Diagnosis Output

Every project should produce this short summary:

```md
Brand mode:
Primary CTA:
Audience:
Asset truth:
Best visual direction:
Required sections:
Optional sections:
Remove/avoid:
Motion style:
```
