# Demo vs Paid Build Rules

Not every website needs the same level of build depth.

## Demo Site

Goal:

- impress the owner
- show the business potential
- start a conversation

Target time:

- 2 to 4 hours once systems exist

Include:

- strong hero
- real business info
- real photos
- services/menu
- proof if it improves the site
- location/contact
- one clear CTA
- polished mobile view

Do not overbuild:

- real backend booking
- complex CMS
- excessive animation
- full legal setup
- advanced SEO beyond basics

## Sales Demo

Goal:

- show a more serious version after interest

Target time:

- 1 day

Include:

- stronger copy
- more careful mobile polish
- better SEO basics
- working mock flows
- clearer handoff path

## Paid Build

Goal:

- production-ready website

Include:

- final copy approved by owner
- real assets approved
- full SEO basics
- analytics/search console
- performance optimization
- accessibility checks
- proper booking/contact integration
- legal/privacy where needed
- client handover

## Hard Rule

Do not spend paid-build effort on cold outreach demos unless the piece itself becomes marketing content.
