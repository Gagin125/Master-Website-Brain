# Project Workflow

Use this workflow for real websites and demo websites.

## Step 1 - Gather Facts

Get business data, services, prices, hours, contact details, booking links, social links, and reviews.

## Step 2 - Inspect Assets

Look at photos and videos before deciding design. Real assets should drive the visual direction.

## Step 3 - Diagnose Brand

Use [[02 Brand Diagnosis Framework]].

Do not skip this.

## Step 4 - Choose Section Candidates

Pick possible sections based on user goal and business type.

Candidate does not mean required.

## Step 5 - Cut Sections

Remove anything that does not improve:

- clarity
- trust
- conversion
- brand feeling
- SEO
- user confidence

## Step 6 - Choose Visual System

Decide:

- typeface direction
- color system
- spacing rhythm
- corner radius
- image treatment
- motion style
- CTA hierarchy

## Step 7 - Plan Before Coding

Write a short locked plan:

- sections in order
- visual direction
- primary CTA behavior
- mobile behavior
- what will not be touched

## Step 8 - Build First Version

Use existing stack. Prefer simple, fast, reliable code.

For demos, do not overbuild advanced systems unless they directly sell the value.

## Step 9 - QA

Check:

- desktop
- laptop
- tablet
- phone
- mobile nav
- language switch if present
- forms or booking flow
- image loading
- links
- section rhythm
- visual bugs

## Step 10 - Critique Before Delivery

Act like a harsh creative director.

Ask:

- What feels generic?
- What feels fake?
- What section should be removed?
- What is visually inconsistent?
- What is too slow?
- What will the business owner notice first?

## Step 11 - Retrospective

After each project, write:

- what worked
- what failed
- what was rejected
- what rules were learned
