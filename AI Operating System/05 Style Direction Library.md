# Style Direction Library

Use style directions as starting points, not templates.

The brand diagnosis decides which direction applies.

## Luxury Editorial

Use for:

- premium restaurants
- fashion
- high-end grooming
- boutique hospitality

Traits:

- serif display type
- restrained sans body
- strong whitespace
- black, ivory, taupe, stone
- real photography
- slow, deliberate motion

Avoid:

- fake gold without brand support
- too many cards
- playful blobs

## Warm Local

Use for:

- cafes
- bakeries
- family shops
- cozy local businesses

Traits:

- cream, espresso, warm gray, muted green
- friendly copy
- real interior/product photos
- soft section rhythm
- readable menu/pricing

Avoid:

- cold black everywhere
- overly corporate layout
- heavy luxury language

## Clean Modern Service

Use for:

- clinics
- salons
- barbershops
- local service providers

Traits:

- crisp layout
- strong hierarchy
- clear services/prices
- real people
- simple CTA path

Avoid:

- vague brand copy
- overanimated effects
- fake personality

## Urban / Street

Use for:

- youth-focused barbershops
- tattoo studios
- streetwear
- nightlife

Traits:

- dark canvas
- bold type
- tighter energy
- high contrast
- candid photos
- sharper motion

Avoid:

- soft cafe palette
- overly polite copy
- generic luxury serif everywhere

## Playful Youth

Use for:

- donuts
- ice cream
- candy
- kids/family offers
- colorful brands

Traits:

- vivid but controlled colors
- bolder shapes
- kinetic interaction
- joyful product photography

Avoid:

- serious editorial tone
- muted everything
- motion that hides the product

## Minimal Professional

Use for:

- consultants
- B2B services
- agencies
- technical businesses

Traits:

- restrained palette
- strong copy
- clear proof
- no decoration without purpose

Avoid:

- excessive hero drama
- vague "premium" visuals with no substance
