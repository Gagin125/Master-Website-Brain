# Section Decision System

Sections are candidates, not obligations.

Do not include a section because it appears in a template.

## Hero

Use when:

- always, unless building a tiny utility page

Must answer:

- what is this
- where is it if local
- why trust it
- what should I do next

Avoid:

- vague slogans
- two equal CTAs
- hero text that hides the real offer
- background media that weakens readability

## Trust Strip

Use when:

- numbers are strong and quick to understand
- it does not interrupt visual flow
- it builds trust faster than photos or reviews

Remove when:

- it feels like SaaS filler
- it breaks the hero-to-next-section rhythm
- reviews, photos, or team already prove trust better

## Team

Use when:

- users choose a specific person
- service quality depends on individual trust
- real team photos exist

Avoid:

- stuffing services/prices inside team cards
- heavy buttons under every person if booking happens later
- generic bios

## Services / Menu / Pricing

Use when:

- users need to compare options before booking/buying

Prefer:

- single-column lists for premium clarity
- tabs only when categories are meaningful
- prices aligned cleanly
- short descriptions only where helpful

Avoid:

- uneven two-column layouts that break row rhythm
- too many categories
- repeated footnotes under every item

## Results / Gallery

Use when:

- visual proof matters
- real photos are strong

Prefer:

- real work clearly visible
- carousel or masonry only when it suits the assets
- no decorative gallery if it does not prove anything

Avoid:

- stock photos
- repeated images
- images cropped so the product/result is invisible

## Reviews

Use when:

- reviews are specific, believable, and improve trust
- they can be designed without feeling pasted in

Remove when:

- the section looks generic
- the design is stronger without it
- reviews repeat what photos already prove

## FAQ

Use when:

- it removes booking/buying friction
- customers commonly ask these things

Keep it short.

Avoid:

- questions invented just to fill space
- cheap accordion animation
- overly explanatory helper text

## Location / Contact

Use when:

- local business

Must include:

- address
- hours
- phone/email if available
- map or directions
- social links if relevant

Avoid:

- default map brightness clashing with dark design
- scattered contact details
- huge gaps between phone, buttons, and social links

## Booking / Final CTA

Use when:

- appointment, reservation, quote, or order is the primary action

Make it feel like the final destination of the page.

Avoid:

- too many secondary CTAs
- button styles that make all actions look equal
- complex flow in cold demos unless it is the main selling point
