# Hero Sections

The hero must do real work.

## Required Jobs

Answer:

- what business is this
- where is it if local
- who is it for
- why trust it
- what should I do now

## Primary CTA

Use one dominant primary CTA.

Secondary CTA only if it solves a different real intent and does not compete.

## Copy Rules

Avoid vague slogans.

Good hero copy is specific:

- business type
- location
- user benefit
- action

## Visual Rules

Choose media based on brand truth:

- restaurant: atmosphere/product/video
- cafe: product/interior
- barbershop: result, barber, or interior with strong identity
- local service: real people/proof

If the image does not support the copy, change the image.

## Motion

Use staggered reveal:

1. headline
2. supporting copy
3. CTA

Keep it slow enough to feel intentional, not delayed.

## Avoid

- background image that makes text hard to read
- CTA too small compared with headline
- animated media that loads late with no poster
- hero text split into too many lines
- two equal buttons
