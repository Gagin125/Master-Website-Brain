# Team Sections

Team sections should build human trust.

## Use When

- customers choose a person
- personal trust matters
- real team photos exist

## Strong Pattern

- real portrait
- name
- role
- one short quote or proof line

Keep it editorial and quiet.

## Avoid

- price lists inside team cards
- heavy "choose this person" buttons if booking happens later
- misaligned card heights
- generic bios
- too many labels

## Motion

Better sequence:

1. portraits enter
2. names appear on scroll
3. quotes appear last

This feels more intentional than spawning all content at once.
