# Demo Website Rules

Demo websites have a special problem: they must feel real, but they are not official until approved.

## Before Owner Approval

- It is okay to create a realistic structure.
- It is okay to use public business facts carefully.
- Avoid claiming official ownership.
- Avoid fake reviews or fake awards.
- Avoid publishing unapproved claims.
- Use real business contact info only when the owner approves publishing.
- Before launch, confirm all copy, prices, hours, reviews, photos, legal pages, and contact details with the owner.

## After Owner Approval

- Remove demo disclaimers if the site becomes official.
- Add real analytics/search console.
- Add real structured data.
- Submit sitemap.
- Confirm Google Business Profile website link.

## Demo Design Rule

A demo site should look like a polished real website, not like a fake mockup.

But do not cross the line into pretending the site is official before the owner approves it.

## Demo Asset Rule

Real photos, public reviews, and public business facts can make a demo more persuasive, but they must be handled carefully.

If the site is going live:

- Confirm permission for photos.
- Confirm permission for review usage.
- Confirm prices and hours.
- Confirm contact details.
- Confirm social links.
- Confirm all claims.
