## Core Idea

Creative professionals should not behave like order-taking vendors. They should specialize, diagnose before prescribing, and sell expertise instead of free execution.

For the AI website brain, this matters because the AI should not jump straight into building. It should first understand the business problem, positioning, audience, and strategic goal.

## Key Principles

### 1. Specialization creates power

Trying to serve everyone makes a creative business interchangeable.

For website strategy:

- a business should not try to attract everyone
- a website should make positioning clear
- specificity attracts better-fit customers
- generic design invites price comparison

### 2. Diagnose before prescribing

Do not provide a design solution before understanding the problem.

Website translation:

Before design, clarify:

- what business are we improving?
- what is the main conversion?
- who is the customer?
- what are they skeptical about?
- what is the current bottleneck?
- what proof exists?
- what assets exist?
- what must be avoided?

### 3. Expertise is different from execution

Execution asks, "What do you want me to make?"

Expertise asks, "What should be made to solve the problem?"

For AI agents:

- do not blindly follow bad design prompts
- explain risks when user requests may hurt clarity
- propose better structure when needed
- preserve user intent while improving execution

### 4. Strategy before creative development

Creative work should begin after the challenge is diagnosed and strategic direction is agreed.

In website workflow:

1. research/context
2. positioning
3. information architecture
4. content requirements
5. design direction
6. build
7. test
8. optimize

Skipping strategy creates pretty but weak sites.

### 5. Proof supports positioning

Positioning is not only a claim. It needs evidence.

Website examples:

- reviews
- portfolio
- case studies
- real photos
- menu/pricing
- team
- process
- location
- credentials

### 6. Control the process

The expert should guide the engagement. This is not about ego; it is about preventing chaotic decision-making.

AI collaboration translation:

- plan before editing when user asks to plan
- make scoped changes when user asks for scoped changes
- avoid redesigning entire sites unless asked
- use checklists for agreed work
- verify before final

### 7. Price and value depend on differentiation

If a business looks like every competitor, users compare on price.

Website translation:

- show specific difference
- show proof of value
- avoid generic category language
- express the brand's real point of view

## Website Translation

### For demo websites

Do not build generic demo sites. Use public info to create a specific, plausible direction, but be clear about what must be confirmed before launch.

### For client checklists

Ask for information that supports strategy:

- business goal
- target customer
- offer/menu/services
- proof/reviews
- real photos
- practical contact info
- brand preferences
- must-avoid items

### For AI-agent work

The agent should behave like:

- strategic companion during planning
- disciplined engineer during execution

Not like:

- random template generator
- yes-man
- uncontrolled redesign machine

## Common AI Website Mistakes

- building before diagnosis
- assuming "premium" is the strategy
- treating client wishes as automatically correct
- redesigning beyond scope
- failing to ask what outcome matters
- ignoring differentiation
- using generic proof instead of specific proof

## Candidate Rules For The Brain

- Diagnose before prescribing.
- Strategy must precede design.
- Specific positioning beats broad appeal.
- Expertise means guiding decisions, not obeying every surface request.
- The website should prove the positioning.
- Scope control is part of professionalism.
- Generic design creates price competition.

## Rules Not To Overgeneralize

- Do not become arrogant in the name of expertise.
- Do not ignore user/client preferences.
- Do not overspecialize a small local business into something false.
- Do not turn every project into a strategy marathon when the need is simple.
