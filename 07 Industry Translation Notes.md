# Industry Translation Notes

## Cafe / Small Food Place

Priorities:

- Real food and interior photos.
- Menu preview.
- Location and hours.
- Friendly brand warmth.
- Mobile-first visit planning.

Avoid:

- Overly corporate tone.
- Too many decorative chips.
- Stock food images.

## Luxury Restaurant

Priorities:

- Atmosphere.
- Reservation.
- Editorial typography.
- High-quality imagery/video.
- Menu detail.
- Events/private dining.

Avoid:

- Emojis.
- Generic cards.
- Cluttered CTA rows.
- Fast/loud animations.

## Barber Shop

Priorities:

- Style confidence.
- Booking.
- Real cuts and interior.
- Price/service clarity.
- Trust in consistency.

Premium can mean:

- Dark editorial lookbook.
- Clean service selector.
- Strong booking CTA.

## Plumber / Electrician

Priorities:

- Fast contact.
- Service area.
- Emergency clarity.
- Trust proof.
- Simple service categories.
- Phone-first CTA.

Premium does not mean:

- Fashion lookbook.
- Massive abstract hero.
- Decorative animation.

Premium here means:

- Clean, credible, fast, organized, trustworthy.
