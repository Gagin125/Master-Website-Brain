# Cafes And Bakeries Decision System

This is not a template. Diagnose the specific cafe first.

## Default User Goal

Visitors usually want:

- vibe
- menu
- location
- opening hours
- photos of food/drinks/interior
- reviews

## Primary CTA Options

Choose one:

- view menu
- visit us / directions
- order
- reserve if applicable

## Candidate Sections

Usually useful:

- hero
- menu highlights
- atmosphere/gallery
- reviews
- location/contact
- footer

Optional:

- kids/family section
- full menu
- story/about
- team

## Visual Directions

### Warm Local

Use for cozy cafes with real interior and product photos.

### Editorial Minimal

Use for specialty coffee brands with strong identity and clean assets.

### Playful Product

Use for ice cream, donuts, colorful desserts, or youth-focused cafes.

## Rules

- Real photos beat stock photos.
- Menu should be readable, not decorative only.
- Do not show Google/customer photos without owner approval for published work.
- If public demo uses public material, treat it as temporary demo material and replace before launch.
- Reviews can work well, but design them as editorial proof, not generic cards.

## Avoid

- stock cafe imagery
- too many decorative pills
- generic "cozy moments" copy with no specific proof
- hiding hours/location
- dark luxury tone if the brand is warm and local
